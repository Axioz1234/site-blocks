T121

Callout boxes

Four tinted variants: Note, Tip, Warning, and Danger. Each has a left accent rail, an icon, a bold label, and a single body line. Pure CSS.

EXAMPLE
ℹ
Note

Standard delivery is free Australia-wide. Express is $16.95 if the moon won't wait.

✓
Tip

One card a month is the whole practice. There's no streak to keep.

▲
Warning

We deliver within Australia only. The checkout won't accept an overseas address.

✕
Danger

If unsure, or if burning paper is not permitted, do not light it. Tear the card instead.
