On this page Before you write The ritual Fire safety
Before you write

Find ten quiet minutes on the night of the full moon. Check the locations index for your month's landmark, put the kettle on, and settle in. Scroll this box and watch the rail follow.

The ritual

Write what you are ready to let go of on the card's writable side. One line or the whole space, both count. Then choose your release: burn it or tear it up.

Fire safety

Burning is optional and for adults. If unsure, or if burning paper is not permitted, do not light it. Tear the card instead.
