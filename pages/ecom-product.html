The ritual

On the night of the full moon, write what you're ready to let go of on the back of that month's card. Then release it.

Burning is optional. Adults only. If you're unsure, or open flames aren't permitted where you are, do not light it. Tear the card instead. A torn card is a complete release.

Delivery and returns

Standard delivery is free Australia wide. Express is $16.95. We post from Maroochydore, Queensland, and email you when your box is on its way.

If your deck arrives damaged, get in touch and we'll sort it out. Nothing here limits your rights under Australian Consumer Law.
