Description

The Wanderer is a heavyweight merino hood built for shoulder season: cold mornings, warm afternoons, and the drive in between. The yarn is spun from 19.5 micron Australian wool and knitted at a family mill in Brunswick.

A two way zip lets you open the hem while seated without losing the collar. The kangaroo pocket is deep enough for a phone and a set of keys, and the cuffs are ribbed tight so they hold a glove line.
