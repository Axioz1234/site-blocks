T076

FAQ, expanded by default

The accordion pattern with every item open on load (set the open attribute). Collapse any you don’t need.

Do I have to burn the card?

No. Burning is optional and for adults only. Tearing the card up is an equally complete release, and the better choice if you're unsure or open flames aren't permitted where you are.

What if I miss a month?

Nothing happens. There's no streak to keep. The next full moon comes around either way, and the card will still be in the box.

Do you ship outside Australia?

Not yet. We deliver Australia-wide, and standard delivery is free.
